Calibration Keeper privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 26 November 2026.

Mizzen Studios provides Calibration Keeper, an Atlassian Forge application for exploring calibration-impact reviews using synthetic demonstration data and permission-aware evidence discovery. This notice describes the current app, including its free installation rehearsal.

Data the app processes

  • Atlassian account IDs used for access checks, quality-manager membership and attribution of review actions.
  • Synthetic instrument/register and review state, decisions, sign-offs, free-text reasons, command receipts and audit history. Decisions and reasons are associated with account IDs. Synthetic records are not a claim that all stored information is anonymous.
  • Installation, site and project context used to isolate data; partition identifiers are hashed, which does not anonymize the account IDs within records.
  • Selected Jira work-item identities, keys and summaries and linked Confluence page identities, titles and URLs visible to the signed-in user. Teamwork Graph results are processed transiently for display, not stored as a report catalogue in app SQL. Native text and user-entered reasons may contain personal data.
  • Bounded operational diagnostics, including identifiers, error/status codes and timings, in Forge developer logs.

Why and where processing happens

Processing enables authorized app access, project-scoped review state, attributable decisions, evidence discovery, troubleshooting and support. Persistent app records are stored in Atlassian Forge SQL. Jira, Confluence, Teamwork Graph and Forge provide the platform services. Evidence discovery runs as the signed-in user, subject to native permissions; it does not use an app-privileged fallback to bypass access controls.

The shipped app has no external egress permissions, external analytics, advertising, external AI/model calls or external data-storage services. It does not sell app data. These are statements about this app's runtime, not a claim that Atlassian has no subprocessors. The app does not request a separate user password or an additional email address.

Retention and deletion limits

Review records, reasons, account IDs and history are durable. No fixed application retention period, automatic erase flow, scheduled retention deletion or Forge personal-data reporting integration has been implemented yet. Storage-capacity limits are not a retention policy. Uninstalling must not be assumed to immediately erase records or backups; underlying retention and backup handling depend on the applicable Atlassian platform services.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, email ollymcbride4@gmail.com and identify the product and site without sending credentials or sensitive evidence. Requests require manual assessment, identity/authority verification and consideration of technical limits and applicable obligations. We do not promise an automatic deletion mechanism or a fixed deletion deadline. This policy is not a certification of regulatory compliance.

Access, demonstration use and support

Use only synthetic, non-sensitive demonstration content. Do not enter real calibration evidence, special-category personal data, secrets or confidential customer material. The app's advisory output is not a calibration certificate or an authorization for real-world safety, release or compliance decisions. Site administrators manage installation and platform permissions.

If you email support, the email address and information you choose to send are processed to respond using ordinary email services; this is separate from the app's no-external-egress runtime. Do not email passwords or API tokens.
