PlanDesk privacy notice

Published 26 November 2026. Publisher and contact: Mizzen Studios, ollymcbride4@gmail.com. This notice describes the current app and documentation site; it is not a certification of compliance or a substitute for an organization's required processing agreement.

Information and purposes

PlanDesk processes plan images, room names/geometry, project/portal/request-type identifiers, configuration and transition/routing settings, actor account IDs/timestamps, issue keys and mapped location/completion metadata. It reads permitted Jira/JSM titles, statuses, request and attachment metadata/content to display work, map location and perform authorized workflow/photo operations. Plan images are held in Forge configuration; uploaded after-photos become native Jira/JSM attachments, not an additional app-owned photo library.

Audit records can contain actor/action/time and relevant issue/configuration/attachment references. Audit lists are bounded to the last 50 entries per key, not a number of days. Operational logs may contain codes, counts/timings and service identifiers. Support correspondence includes your contact details and diagnostics you choose to send. Do not send unnecessary sensitive data.

Hosting and recipients

The app uses Atlassian Forge/KVS and Jira/JSM APIs. Current app code has no app-operated external backend, external fetch allowlist, advertising analytics, data-sale or external LLM/model-training feature. Atlassian platform processing is governed by its applicable arrangements. This public documentation is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Email correspondence uses the contact mailbox's provider. This website adds no analytics, forms, cookies, third-party fonts or scripts.

No app-specific data-residency region, end-to-end encryption, SOC2/ISO certification or independent backup guarantee is offered. Organizations needing particular transfer, residency, subprocessor or contractual safeguards should obtain the applicable platform terms and agreement with Mizzen Studios before production use.

Access and photo sharing

PlanDesk uses trusted Forge invocation identity and server-side binding/role checks. Native Jira/JSM access and attachment sharing still apply, including outside the app panel. Uploaded photos are public request attachments within the Jira/JSM sharing model, not internet-public links or app-private storage. Your site administrator controls native access/participants/organizations. Printed sensitive labels can remain in shared plan images. Image processing does not guarantee EXIF/GPS removal.

Retention, uninstall and rights requests

There is no general app record TTL, scheduled global erasure or self-service global export endpoint. Configuration/job metadata persists according to app/platform lifecycle unless an authorized change is made. Jira fields/properties/attachments follow site controls and can survive uninstall. Platform logs/backups/recovery follow Atlassian's applicable lifecycle; support email follows the mailbox provider and authorized handling. No fixed deletion period across all these systems is promised. Uninstall does not guarantee immediate complete erasure.

Contact your site administrator and Mizzen Studios for access, correction, export or deletion requests and applicable privacy rights. We need to establish requester authority and relevant records; do not send a password or identity document unless a justified secure process is agreed. A request is not completed until the authorized action and its limits are confirmed. Organizations requiring a DPA or specified retention/deletion service should agree it before use; publication of this page does not execute a DPA.

Responsibility and questions

Your organization determines the authorized facilities purpose, content and native site sharing. Applicable controller/processor roles, legal bases and contractual instructions depend on the customer arrangement and applicable law; they are not established merely by Forge hosting or this website. Contact Mizzen Studios to resolve those requirements before using personal or confidential data. Do not upload secrets, unapproved building plans or unnecessary sensitive personal data.

Security and changes

Report vulnerabilities or suspected disclosure privately to the contact email. We do not promise perfect security or a fixed incident-notification deadline; applicable law and contractual duties govern. Material changes to this notice will be dated here. Support hours/target are on the support page.
