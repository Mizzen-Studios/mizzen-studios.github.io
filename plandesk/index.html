PlanDesk

Give facilities requests a place to start.

PlanDesk connects facilities work to a saved room plan in Jira Service Management. Reporters can stage a mapped room on an existing request form; facilities teams can review permitted mapped work by room and Jira status and inspect completion-photo context.

Availability

Published by Mizzen Studios. PlanDesk is being prepared for a Free Marketplace listing; it is not yet Marketplace-approved or generally available. Installation requires an authorized Jira administrator and suitable Jira/JSM access. Free app pricing does not include Atlassian licences or guarantee unlimited capacity.

No purchase, payment method, invitation or installation is offered by this website. Read the setup guide, privacy notice, support information and terms before evaluating the app.

Not an emergency system

Do not rely on PlanDesk for emergencies or safety-critical decisions. No offline mode, guaranteed uptime or guaranteed repair outcome is offered.
