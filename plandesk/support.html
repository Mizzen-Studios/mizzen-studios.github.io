PlanDesk support

Email ollymcbride4@gmail.com for installation help, bugs, privacy requests or security reports.

What to include

App version/environment, browser, steps to reproduce, expected/actual result and a redacted error message. Do not send passwords, tokens, installation links, full building plans, private photos or another customer's records. Security reports should be private, not a public ticket.

Access problems

Ask your site administrator to check Jira/JSM permissions, app environment, project/request-type configuration and available transitions. Do not broaden native sharing simply to make a panel work.

Coverage

Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 UK time (Europe/London). We aim to respond within 48 hours. This is a response target, not an SLA, guaranteed resolution time, emergency service or 24/7 coverage.

Privacy and incidents

Use the same email privately. Site data changes require verified authority. Self-service global export/erasure is not offered. Do not assume email delivery proves a particular response deadline or that a requested deletion has happened without confirmation.
