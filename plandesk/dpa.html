PlanDesk Data Processing Addendum

Published: 27 November 2026. Supplier: Olly McBride, an individual sole trader in the United Kingdom, trading as Mizzen Studios (not a registered company). The supplier’s contact is ollymcbride4@gmail.com. No home or postal address is published here. Effective date, customer and applicable agreement: [to be agreed for an executed copy]. This addendum covers PlanDesk for Jira Service Management. The judge and Marketplace app identities run the same permission/privacy-fixed code, with separate installations and storage.

1. Roles and instructions

The customer determines the purposes and means of its facilities data and acts as controller, or as an authorised processor instructing the supplier. The supplier acts as processor for app data solely on documented customer instructions to provide, secure and support PlanDesk, unless applicable law requires otherwise. The customer remains responsible for lawful instructions, notices, access settings and an appropriate legal basis. For support correspondence handled through the approved contact email, the supplier acts as an independent controller; that separate flow is outside this processor-service schedule. No product advertising or analytics purpose is authorised by this addendum.

2. Processing schedule

Purpose: map facilities work to configured rooms, stage a location on a native JSM request form, show authorised work and completion context, and administer access and privacy obligations. Duration: the customer’s authorised installation/service period and applicable deletion and retention handling below. Data subjects: customer administrators, reporters, agents and authorised contractors. Data: Atlassian account IDs, plan images and room/location/configuration metadata, request references and audit metadata; permitted native Jira/JSM request fields and attachments. Users must avoid unnecessary sensitive information in plans, filenames and photos. Synthetic sample fixtures are not real customer requests and sample interactions do not persist data.

3. Confidentiality and security

The supplier will restrict access to authorised personnel bound by confidentiality, use proportionate safeguards, and not sell or use app data for unrelated purposes. App code uses Atlassian Forge and Jira/JSM services and their native permissions; attachment visibility follows Jira/JSM sharing. Measures include native user-access checks before interactive app-authorised operations, tenant-scoped Forge storage, periodic account-status reporting and handling of covered closed/updated account references. Olly is the only developer; two-step sign-in is enabled on all accounts. The supplier accepts Atlassian partner security bug-fix and incident-notification obligations. Additional customer-specific measures and assistance procedures may be agreed before execution. No independent certification, end-to-end encryption, safety assessment or universal EXIF removal is promised. The supplier will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting processor-service data and provide reasonably available information and assistance; applicable mandatory obligations control.

4. Hosting and subprocessors

For the app data plane, all persisted processing/storage is within Atlassian Forge and Jira/JSM; client-side processing remains in the end-user browser. No supplier-operated external backend, Remote or external model is part of PlanDesk. Atlassian is the supplier’s sole directly appointed subprocessor for this app-data service. Atlassian may use its own authorised subprocessors under its published terms and list; “no additional direct subprocessor” does not mean Atlassian has no subprocessors. Platform residency, transfers and safeguards follow the applicable Atlassian service arrangements, not an unverified guarantee that a particular site is pinned.

The support mailbox is hosted outside Atlassian and support correspondence is a separate controller flow. Public documentation hosting may process visitor/network information separately. Therefore this addendum does not claim that every company/support/website data flow remains inside Atlassian or has no other provider. Customers should not send plans, photos, credentials or unnecessary personal information by email.

5. Rights, assistance and deletion

The supplier will reasonably assist verified, authorised customer instructions for access, correction, export, erasure, security inquiries and legally required assessments, subject to applicable law and platform capabilities. No unverified requester can authorise another tenant’s data export or deletion. Account reporting and resulting erasure/refresh apply to app-held personal identifiers; they do not automatically delete native Jira issues, customer-owned attachments, history or the customer’s Atlassian account. A runbook or this addendum is not a general self-service export/deletion feature.

6. Retention after uninstall

Current Forge documentation states that Forge-hosted storage is retained for 28 days after uninstallation, with no automatic reattachment on reinstall; recovery requires customer consent and the platform’s applicable recovery process. App records may be erased earlier while authorised access exists. A “0–28 day” window describes possible earlier app-record erasure through the end of the platform retention window, not a configurable uninstall deletion timer, guaranteed immediate erasure or a retention promise for all data. Native Jira/JSM fields, properties, requests, attachments and history follow the customer/platform policy and may remain beyond 28 days; platform logs/backups and separately controlled support mail require their own applicable schedules and legal review. No unconditional complete-erasure date is promised for those separate stores.

7. Supplier details and contractual completion

Olly is the sole developer and the named privacy/security contact through the support email. Two-step sign-in is enabled on all accounts, and the supplier accepts Atlassian partner security bug-fix and incident-notification obligations. Laptop full-disk encryption is not enabled; the owner reports no app data is stored on that laptop. App data-plane storage is Forge/Jira/JSM; that statement is not an independent device audit or a claim that support/website processing is inside Atlassian. No registered-company number or public home address is supplied. Applicable law, supervisory authority, customer instructions, assistance procedures, transfer/subprocessor notice/objection terms and any executed contractual details still require the appropriate legal confirmation. Mandatory data-protection law prevails over inconsistent language. This draft does not itself establish compliance or override customer/platform rights.
